# SuStash Privacy Policy

*Last updated: July 14, 2026*

SuStash is built around a simple principle: **your saved links are yours.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** SuStash has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
services. We never see your saved links, your collections, or anything else about you.

## Where your data lives

- Everything you save is stored **on your device**.
- If you enable **iCloud Sync** (a SuStash Pro feature), your saved links are stored in
  your personal, private iCloud database, managed by Apple and protected by your Apple
  Account. We have no access to it.
- Smart features — automatic organization, suggestions, and search — run entirely
  **on your device** using Apple's on-device machine learning. Nothing is sent anywhere
  for processing.

## Network requests SuStash makes

When you save a link, SuStash contacts **the website you saved** to fetch its title,
preview image, and (for products) price — the same way a browser would when opening the
page. These requests go directly from your device to that website. No intermediary
servers are involved.

## Purchases

SuStash Pro purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no
personal or payment information about you.

## Notifications

The optional weekly reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It is off
by default and can be turned off at any time in SuStash Settings.

## Data deletion

Delete any link, any collection, or everything at once from within the app
(Settings → Delete all data). If iCloud Sync is enabled, deletions propagate to your
private iCloud database. Deleting the app removes all local data.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a
new date. Since SuStash collects nothing, changes are unlikely to affect you.
